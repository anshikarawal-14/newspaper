ENVIRONMENT
The environment is the complex system of all living (biotic) and non-living (abiotic) things that surround an organism or community, influencing its survival, growth, and daily life. Types of Environment • Natural Environment: The physical, chemical, and biological world formed without major human intervention, including the atmosphere, oceans, forests, deserts, rivers, and wildlife. • Human-Made (Anthropogenic) Environment: Landscapes and structures modified or built by humans, such as cities, farms, roads, and industrial zones. • Computing Environment: A digital or virtual setting (like a software workspace or operating system) where tasks or programs are run.
